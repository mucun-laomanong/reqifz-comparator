- Free to use, modify, and distribute.

## Who Needs a Commercial License?

You need a commercial license if **any** of the following applies:

- You want to integrate the software into a closed-source product or service.
- You want to use the software internally without releasing your modifications under the AGPL-3.0.
- Your organization has **10 or more employees and contractors** and you do not wish to comply with the AGPL-3.0.

You do **not** need a commercial license if:

- You are an individual using the software for personal or non-commercial purposes.
- Your organization has **fewer than 10 employees and contractors** and you do not wish to comply with the AGPL-3.0.
- You are willing to comply with the AGPL-3.0 (i.e. release your modifications under the same license).

## Small Team Exemption

Organizations with **fewer than 10 employees and contractors** may request a **free commercial license**. This exemption applies to the entire organization, including all subsidiaries and affiliates that share the same majority ownership.

To request the exemption, please contact us with the following information:

- Your organization's name.
- The total number of employees and contractors.
- A short description of how you intend to use the software.

A commercial license typically includes:

- **Non-exclusive, non-transferable right** to use, modify, and distribute the software in binary form without source code disclosure.
- **Perpetual or subscription-based** terms (to be agreed upon).
- **Warranty** that the software will perform substantially in accordance with its documentation.
- **Indemnification** against third-party intellectual property claims arising from the software as provided by us.
- **Priority support** and **maintenance** (optional, may be subject to additional fees).

The exact terms are set out in a separate written agreement signed by both parties.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use the software for free in my company if we have fewer than 10 people?**  
A: Yes. You may either comply with the AGPL-3.0, or request a free commercial license under the small team exemption.

**Q: Can I use the software for free if my company has more than 10 people?**  
A: Yes, if you comply with the AGPL-3.0. If you do not want to comply with the AGPL-3.0, you must purchase a commercial license.

**Q: Does the commercial license cover all subsidiaries?**  
A: The small team exemption applies to the entire organization, including subsidiaries and affiliates under common majority ownership. For a paid commercial license, the scope is defined in the agreement.

**Q: What counts as an "employee or contractor"?**  
A: Any individual who works for your organization on a full-time, part-time, or contract basis, including freelancers and consultants.

**Q: I am an individual developer. Do I need a commercial license?**  
A: No. You can use the software for free under the AGPL-3.0.

**Q: Can I redistribute the software under a different license?**  
A: Only if you have a commercial license that explicitly permits it. Otherwise, the AGPL-3.0 applies.
